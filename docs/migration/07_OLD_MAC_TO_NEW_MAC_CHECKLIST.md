## Move by hand — 1 optional item

| # | What | From (old Mac) | To (new Mac) | Mandatory? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The private handoff **directory** | `~/Desktop/UMRAH_CONNECT_PRIVATE_MIGRATION/` | anywhere on the new Mac (e.g. `~/Desktop/`) | **Optional** |

Move it with AirDrop, an encrypted USB stick, or iCloud Drive.
**Do not** email it, put it in a shared/public folder, or place it inside the
repository working tree.

### Why it is optional

Its two files are `platform/api/.env` and `apps/web/.env.local`, and both hold
**only development placeholders**. The committed `.env.example` templates
reproduce them — and in the API's case the template is *better*, because the
live file predates three variables and points at the non-standard port 5433.

**Recommended:** ignore the handoff and use the templates.

```bash
cp platform/api/.env.example platform/api/.env
cp apps/web/.env.local.example apps/web/.env.local
```

Take the handoff only if you specifically want to see what the old machine ran.
